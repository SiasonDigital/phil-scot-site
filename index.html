About PHIL+SCOT SOLUTIONS LTD

From 2016 to 2018 PHIL+SCOT SOLUTIONS LTD ran a UK distributorship, sourcing industrial products, bidding for tenders and running the operations behind every order. That work now sits beside five more service lines.

Six services from one company. Ask for one or several.

  • Engineering consultancy and services

    Specifications, product data sheets and tender support for oilfield and industrial equipment.

  • Product distribution

    Sourcing and supplying industrial and oilfield products to UK buyers, from quotation and tender to delivery.

  • Property services and management

    Tenancies, repairs and rent accounts for residential and commercial property.

  • Creative and digital arts

    Websites, short explainer videos and product graphics for small firms.

  • Business process services

    Mapping how orders, materials and documents move through a firm, and cutting the re-typing.

  • Data and IT services

    SAP material master clean-up, SQL reports and Power BI dashboards.
